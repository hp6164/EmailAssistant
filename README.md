# Summer (Your AI email secratary)

Convert your 100 unread emails to an easily digestible organized list of 10 notes.
The AI email assistant is a powerful tool that can help you manage your inbox more 
efficiently. It can summarize your emails, identify important messages, and reduce the time 
you spend sifting.

### Market Fit
    In 2023, there were around 3.5 billion email users globally, and about 
    300 billion emails are sent daily.
    On average Business Professionals spend 3 hours checking emails, once every 
    37 min & spend another 2.5 hours outside of business hours checking their personal emails.
    
    By summarizing the user's inbox, we could save users an average of 4.5 hours a day,
    and giver employers an average of 3 more productive hours per employee per day - reducing
    costs of operation by at least $39 per employee per day.

    Business 68% of market
    Personal 32% of market

### Scalability
    After aquiriging 100,000 users, the AI Email Assistant can be scaled up to enterprise application 
    where a large volume of emails from multiple users can be summarized for employer oversight.

### Revenue Potential

    SAM: $12.60 B    (North America, Europe, and APAC)

#### TAM (Total Addressable Market)
    TAM represents the overall revenue opportunity for AI email assistants globally, including
    all potential users (both business and personal).

    Global workforce size: In 2023, there were around 3.5 billion email users globally, and
    about 300 billion emails are sent daily. Considering that a large portion of these emails
    are for business purposes, many professionals could benefit from AI-powered assistants​
    
    MARKET.US
    .
    Market size: In 2023, the AI-powered email assistant market was valued at $285 million,
    and the market is expected to grow at a CAGR of 9.8%. If growth continues, the market
    could expand to $585 million by 2030​.
    
    MARKET.US
    .
    Using these estimates, TAM can be calculated based on global email users and the overall
    projected market for AI-based solutions.

    TAM calculation (for business users):
    If 40% of professionals adopt AI email assistants in the future, this would translate to
    roughly 1.4 billion users globally. Assuming each user generates $10-20 annually from AI
    email assistant subscriptions or SaaS products, the TAM could be:

    TAM = 1.4 billion * $15 (average annual revenue per user)
    TAM = $21 billion

#### SAM (Serviceable Available Market)
    . If you target only businesses in key regions (North America, Europe, and APAC), the SAM
    would represent a subset of the global market.
    Assuming 60% of the total TAM (businesses in these regions) are your target:

    SAM = 0.60 * TAM
    SAM = $12.6 billion

#### SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)
    3% of the SAM:

    SOM = 0.03 * SAM
