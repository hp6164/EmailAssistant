Executive Summary
Penpal provides AI-powered email management, targeting busy professionals overwhelmed by email. The solution automatically summarizes emails and categorizes them for ease of use.

Market Analysis
- Total addressable market: Email clients and enterprise users.
- Customer persona: Entrepreneurs, executives, and employees who rely on email communication.

Revenue Model
Freemium tier with limited AI summaries. Premium tier offers advanced AI summaries, personalized categories, and integrations for $9.99/month.

Go-To-Market Strategy
- Beta testing with early adopters.
- Partnering with email services (Gmail, Outlook).
- Offering the service to corporations with high email volumes.
