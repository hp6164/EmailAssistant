# User Personas

## Persona 1: Busy Professional - Sarah
- **Age**: 35  
- **Job**: Marketing Director  
- **Pain Points**: Hundreds of unread emails every day, difficult to find important ones.
- **Penpal Benefit**: Quickly reviews summaries and prioritizes important emails.

## Persona 2: Freelancer - Alex
- **Age**: 29  
- **Job**: Freelance Developer  
- **Pain Points**: Misses important client emails amidst newsletters and other updates.
- **Penpal Benefit**: Automatically categorizes client communications in the "urgent" category.
