# Partnership Proposal for Email Service Providers

## Objective
Penpal aims to enhance user productivity by integrating its AI email summarization and categorization service with Gmail and Outlook.

## Partnership Value
- Improved email experience for users with AI-driven summaries.
- Increased engagement with email services through better inbox management.

## Collaboration Timeline
- Initial integration within 3 months.
- Shared development roadmap for enhanced features.
